We are delighted to announce that Blue Point Art has received a **grant from the Polonia Aid Foundation Trust (PAFT)** in support of the first edition of *Literature in the Visual Field*.

The project brings literature into direct dialogue with visual art, sound and the gallery space as part of Jarosław Solecki’s exhibition *Imponderabilia*. It will take place at POSK Gallery in London in August 2026.

We are sincerely grateful to the PAFT Board of Trustees for its decision and for the confidence it has shown in our work. This support will help us bring together writers, artists and audiences, and create a shared space for encounters between the written word and contemporary visual practice.

On behalf of everyone involved in the project, **thank you to PAFT for this generous support**.
