This page brings together the surviving public materials from the first edition of **Literature in the Visual Field / Literatura w świecie wizualnym**, presented alongside Jarosław Solecki’s exhibition *Imponderabilia* at POSK Gallery, London, from 16 to 21 August 2026.

The programme brought writers, performers and visitors into the gallery, where readings and conversations shared space with Solecki’s installation. This archive preserves the project’s printed materials and selected media files for future reference.

## A second week in the gallery

The exhibition was originally planned to run for one week, but POSK Gallery kindly offered us a second week. I kept the main exhibition in place but changed the video presentation: instead of two large-scale projections, a single screen showed *The Fields of Vision* alongside recorded poetry readings by the participating authors. The recordings played on a continuous loop throughout the second week of the exhibition.

Special thanks to Joanna Ciechanowska, Director of POSK Gallery, for making this possible.
