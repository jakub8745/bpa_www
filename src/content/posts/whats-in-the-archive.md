**What’s in the Archive?**

* **85 lat PUNO** – An exhibition marking 85 years of the Polish University Abroad.
* **Dialogi Vincenza – spojrzenia** – This show invites you on a captivating journey into the Hutsul region, seen through the lens of Lidio Cipriani.
* **Joanna Ciechanowska – Lockdowns** – Reflecting on life and art in times of isolation.
* **The Virtual Biennale Prague (11th Biennale: Wake-Up Call)** – Artists confront social issues like discrimination and fake news, both globally and locally. Striking black-and-white posters, a Biennale hallmark, provide a sharp critique of contemporary life.

We’re currently working on the next addition, an exhibition themed around **‘Identity’**. More updates soon!
